Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Flow Shorts Factory ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application and its integration with TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we may receive authentication tokens necessary to publish videos on your behalf. We only request permissions essential for publishing media and retrieving upload statuses.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to provide, operate, and maintain the service—specifically to facilitate video generation, rendering, and authorized publishing to connected platforms.

3. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely in encrypted or restricted local storage environments. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

You can disconnect your account at any time via the application settings or directly through your TikTok account settings under Authorized Apps.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our official repository or support channels.
